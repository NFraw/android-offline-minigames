Offline learning resources, v3.7.0, 2026-10-16

Poetry: classical public-domain works, selected from chinese-poetry / qianjiashi.json.
https://github.com/chinese-poetry/chinese-poetry
Source dataset MIT; traditional characters converted to simplified for game content; deduplicated by first line. Retained original 24 learning pairs take priority.
Pinyin: mozillazg/pinyin-data, MIT, https://github.com/mozillazg/pinyin-data
Selected common single-reading characters; tones retained in explanations; umlaut spelling restored after j/q/x. Multireading bare characters excluded.
English vocabulary: project-authored bilingual list of 320 concrete concepts. Pictures generated with OpenAI built-in imagegen, 5 atlases, exact indices in tools/learning-sources/english-visual-concepts.json.
Piano: piano/C4.wav,D4.wav,E4.wav,F4.wav,G4.wav,A4.wav,B4.wav from Nicholas Brosowsky's tonejs-instruments sample collection (piano source identified as VSO2 by sample-source-info.txt).
https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments
Samples licensed CC BY 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
Modified for this project: mono mix, resampled to 44.1kHz PCM16, normalized, trimmed to 0.9 seconds with 0.22-second fade-out. Original source recordings retained under tools/learning-sources.
Generated curling and Chinese chess piece atlases: OpenAI built-in imagegen, project prompts retained in docs/development/题库与美术扩充.md. No gameplay network access is required.
